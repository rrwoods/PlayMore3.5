[COMMON] Unarmed strikes

With at least one hand free, the attack generation routine should generate an unarmed strike attack.  Assume the DM will allow you to two-hand a weapon and make an unarmed strike in the same full attack routine (that is, your offhand still counts as free when only weilding one weapon).

If we strictly follow this method, the UI will basically always be flooded with two-weapon stuff (since either you can attack with an offhand weapon, or you can attack unarmed).  We might want to give the player the option to hide unarmed attacks if they're not interested in them.  Alternatively, make the player "equip" the unarmed strike attack, but this has the drawback of not supporting two-handed-weapon-plus-unarmed.

[USABILITY] If an effect hits every skill, don't count it as relevant

Basically, what happens is if you are (for example) sickened, or defy a vestige, or something else that affects every skill check, the skills list explodes to include every skill.  Those effects shouldn't cause the Relevant function to return true.

Optionally, also show those effects under the skills list.
