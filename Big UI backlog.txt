Ability to create effects.

... there's a lot here

One option is to do a whole UI system based around dropdowns and fields and stuff, making the user choose every fiddly detail.  This is the easier option in terms of what needs to be done to solve the problem, but it is extremely tedious for both the user and the designer.

The other option is to try to parse something the player types.  That is, try to understand things like "+1 morale to attack rolls" and convert them into effects.  Much harder from a design standpoint.  Much, much less tedious for the end user.

And from effect creation, flows many things.
