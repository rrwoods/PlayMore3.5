[ODRIC 3] Feats with multiple different effects

Mostly, to facilitate feats that give you an option.  These will usually require an always-on effect that presents the option, and a toggleable effect that represents what happens when you use the option.  For example, Constant Guardian should have an always-on Def Options text effect, and a toggleable effect that subtracts from your attack rolls.
