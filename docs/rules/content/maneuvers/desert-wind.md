# Desert Wind Maneuvers and Stances

Primary Source: *Tome of Battle: The Book of Nine Swords*, Chapter 4.

### Blistering Flourish (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 52
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: Swordsage 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: You generate a burst of brilliant light. Any creature other than you within 30 ft. must make a Fortitude save (DC 11 + your Wis modifier) or be dazzled for 1 minute.

### Burning Blade (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 52
- **Type**: Boost [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: For the rest of your turn, your melee attacks deal an extra 1d6 points of fire damage + 1 point per initiator level.

### Burning Brand (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 52
- **Type**: Boost [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Your weapon turns into a burning brand for the rest of your turn. The brand increases your reach by 5 feet, and your melee attacks made with the brand deal fire damage equal to your normal melee damage.

### Death Mark (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 52
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, fire erupts from your enemy's body in a spread centered on the creature, dealing 6d6 points of fire damage to all creatures in the area (including the target), with a Reflex save (DC 13 + your Wis modifier) for half damage. The radius is 5 ft. for Small or smaller targets, 10 ft. for Medium, 20 ft. for Large, 30 ft. for Huge, 40 ft. for Gargantuan, or 50 ft. for Colossal. You are immune to this fire damage.

### Desert Tempest
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 52
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: Swordsage 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You move up to your speed. Each time you exit a square adjacent to an enemy, you can first make a single melee attack against that foe. You cannot attack a single enemy more than once with this maneuver.

### Distracting Ember (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 52
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: Swordsage 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Conjure a Small fire elemental (MM 99) in any space within 30 ft. It remains until the end of your turn and threatens all creatures within its reach, but takes no actions or attacks of opportunity.

### Dragon's Flame (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 53
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You blast forth a 30 ft. cone of fire that deals 6d6 points of fire damage. Creatures in the area can attempt Reflex saves (DC 15 + your Wis modifier) for half damage.

### Fan the Flames (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 53
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Desert Wind maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a ranged touch attack against a target within 30 ft. If it hits, the target takes 6d6 points of fire damage.

### Fiery Assault (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 53
- **Type**: Stance [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Your melee attacks deal an extra 1d6 points of fire damage.

### Fire Riposte (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 53
- **Type**: Counter [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: One Desert Wind maneuver
- **Mechanics**: When a creature successfully strikes you with a melee or natural weapon, you can make a melee touch attack against it. If it hits, the target takes 4d6 points of fire damage.

### Firesnake (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 53
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You cause a 60 ft. line of fire to twist across an area to burn your enemies. Unlike a standard line, a firesnake can bend and twist to hit your foes. A firesnake starts in an adjacent square. It can move up to 60 feet each round. It can move out of your line of sight or line of effect, but you gain no special knowledge of the areas it moves into. If a firesnake moves into a creature's space, that creature takes 6d6 points of fire damage, with a Reflex save (DC 14 + your Wis modifier) for half damage. A creature can only take damage from the firesnake once in a single round. It makes the saving throw and takes damage the first time that the firesnake touches it. A firesnake must move along the ground. It cannot fly, and it pays the standard movement penalties for difficult terrain and other effects.

### Flame's Blessing (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 53
- **Type**: Stance [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: You gain fire resistance based on your ranks in Tumble: resistance 5 at 4–8 ranks, 10 at 9–13 ranks, 20 at 14–18 ranks, or immunity to fire at 19+ ranks.

### Flashing Sun
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 54
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: Swordsage 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: One Desert Wind maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a full attack using only melee weapons. You gain one additional attack at your highest attack bonus, but all attacks you make this round take a –2 penalty.

### Hatchling's Flame (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 54
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Desert Wind maneuver
- **Mechanics**: You blast forth a 30 ft. cone of fire that deals 2d6 points of fire damage, with a Reflex save (DC 12 + your Wis modifier) for half damage.

### Holocaust Cloak (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 54
- **Type**: Stance [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Desert Wind maneuver
- **Mechanics**: An adjacent opponent that hits you with a melee attack takes 5 points of fire damage. This does not harm a creature using a reach weapon to attack you.

### Inferno Blade (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 54
- **Type**: Boost [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: For the rest of your turn, your melee attacks deal an extra 3d6 points of fire damage + 1 point per initiator level.

### Inferno Blast (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 55
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 9
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Five Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You create a 60-ft.-radius burst of fire centered on you that deals 100 points of fire damage to all other creatures in the area, with a Reflex save (DC 19 + your Wis modifier) for half damage.

### Leaping Flame (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 55
- **Type**: Counter [Teleport]
- **Level**: Swordsage 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When a creature attacks you with a melee or ranged attack, you instantly teleport up to 100 feet into an unoccupied space adjacent to that attacker after resolving the attack. If you cannot move adjacent to the target, the maneuver fails.

### Lingering Inferno (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 55
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 2d6 points of fire damage, and the target takes an extra 2d6 points of fire damage at the start of each of its turns for 3 rounds.

### Ring of Fire (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 55
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You can move up to twice your speed along continuous, solid ground, leaving a trail of flames. If the trail forms a closed area, an inferno erupts within it, dealing 12d6 points of fire damage to all creatures inside with a Reflex save (DC 16 + your Wis modifier) for half damage. The inferno erupts only the first time you form a closed area.

### Rising Phoenix (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 55
- **Type**: Stance [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You gain a fly speed equal to your land speed with perfect maneuverability, but must remain hovering within 10 feet of a solid or liquid surface. If you hover in place and make a full attack, the column of air deals 3d6 points of fire damage to creatures adjacent to or within the column (you are unharmed). If you are ever more than 10 feet above a surface, the stance immediately ends and you fall.

### Salamander Charge (Su)
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 55
- **Type**: Strike [Fire]
- **Level**: Swordsage 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Desert Wind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack. You can change direction freely during the charge and ignore difficult terrain and other creatures in your path. A wall of fire appears in each square along your movement path for up to 5 rounds (or until dismissed as a swift action, or another salamander charge is initiated). A creature standing in the wall takes 6d6 fire damage at the start of its turn, a creature adjacent to the wall takes 3d6 fire damage at the start of its turn, and creatures moving into or through the wall take 6d6 fire damage. The wall does not block line of sight or line of effect.

- **Prerequisites**: One Desert Wind maneuver
