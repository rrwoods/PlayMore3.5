# Devoted Spirit Maneuvers and Stances

Primary Source: *Tome of Battle: The Book of Nine Swords*, Chapter 4.

### Aura of Chaos
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 57
- **Type**: Stance [Chaos]
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When rolling melee damage, whenever a damage die shows its maximum possible result, reroll it and add the result to the damage total. Continue rerolling and adding as long as any die shows its maximum result.

### Aura of Perfect Order
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 57
- **Type**: Stance [Law]
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Once per round, you can choose to treat any d20 roll (such as an attack, save, or check) as an 11. You must decide to use this ability immediately before rolling the d20.

### Aura of Triumph
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 57
- **Type**: Stance [Good]
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You and each ally within 10 feet heal 4 damage with each successful melee attack either of you makes against an evil target.

### Aura of Tyranny
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 57
- **Type**: Stance [Evil]
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: At the end of your turn, you can deal 2 damage to each willing ally within 10 feet; you heal 1 point of damage for each ally who takes this damage.

### Castigating Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 57
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack against a foe whose alignment differs from yours on at least one component. If it hits, the target takes an extra 8d6 damage and must make a Fortitude save (DC 17 + your Cha modifier) or take a –2 penalty on attack rolls for 1 minute. In addition, all enemies within a 30-ft.-radius burst of the target must make a Fortitude save, taking 5d6 damage and a –2 penalty on attack rolls for 1 minute (a successful save halves damage and negates the penalty).

### Crusader's Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 58
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack against an enemy that poses a threat and whose alignment differs from yours on at least one component. If it hits, you or an ally within 10 feet heals 1d6 damage + 1 point per initiator level (maximum +5).

### Daunting Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 58
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, the target must make a Will save (DC equal to damage dealt) or become shaken for 1 minute.

### Defensive Rebuke
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 58
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Until the start of your next turn, each enemy you strike in melee provokes an attack of opportunity from you each time it attacks anyone other than you in melee.

### Divine Surge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 58
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 8d8 damage.

### Divine Surge, Greater
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 58
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Choose a number X between 0 and your initiator level. Take X Constitution damage. Make a melee attack with a +X bonus on the attack roll. If it hits, you deal an extra 6d8 damage, plus (2 times X)d8 damage. You are flat-footed until the start of your next turn.

### Doom Charge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 58
- **Type**: Strike [Evil]
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver, evil alignment
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack. If the target is good-aligned, you deal an extra 6d6 damage, and on a hit you gain damage reduction 10/— until the start of your next turn.

### Entangling Blade
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 58
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 2d6 damage, and the target's speeds (all movement modes) are reduced by 20 feet until the end of its next turn. A flying creature reduced to 0 ft. fly speed falls.

### Foehammer
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 59
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, it automatically overcomes the target's damage reduction and deals an extra 2d6 damage.

### Immortal Fortitude
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 59
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When damage would reduce you to 0 or fewer hit points, you can make a Fortitude save (DC equal to negative HP total). On a success, you remain alive and conscious at 1 hit point; on a failure, you die or fall unconscious as normal. After 3 saving throw attempts, this stance immediately ends. This does not protect against non-hit-point death effects, petrification, or paralysis.

### Iron Guard's Glare
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 59
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Any opponent you threaten takes a –4 penalty on attack rolls against your other allies.

### Law Bearer
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 59
- **Type**: Strike [Law]
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver, lawful alignment
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack. If the target is chaotic-aligned, you gain a +8 bonus on the attack roll and deal an extra 4d6 damage; on a hit against a chaotic target, you gain a +5 bonus to AC and saves until the start of your next turn.

### Martial Spirit
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 60
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Each time you hit with a melee attack, you or an ally within 30 feet heals 2 damage.

### Radiant Charge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 60
- **Type**: Strike [Good]
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver, good alignment
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack. If the target is evil-aligned, you deal an extra 6d6 damage, and on a hit you gain damage reduction 10/— until the start of your next turn.

### Rallying Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 60
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack against an enemy that poses a threat and whose alignment differs from yours on at least one component. If it hits, you and all allies within 30 feet heal 3d6 damage + 1 point per initiator level (maximum +15).

### Revitalizing Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 60
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack against an enemy that poses a threat and whose alignment differs from yours on at least one component. If it hits, you or an ally within 10 feet heals 3d6 damage + 1 point per initiator level (maximum +10).

### Shield Block
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 60
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: When an opponent attacks an adjacent ally with a melee or ranged attack, you can grant that ally an AC bonus equal to your shield bonus + 4 against that attack. You must choose to use this before knowing whether the attack hits.

### Shield Counter
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 60
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When an opponent you threaten declares an attack (before resolving it), you can make a shield bash attack at a –2 penalty against that opponent. If your shield bash hits, the opponent's attack automatically misses.

### Strike of Righteous Vitality
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 60
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 9
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Devoted Spirit maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack against an enemy that poses a threat and whose alignment differs from yours on at least one component. If it hits, you or one ally within 10 feet gains the benefit of a *heal* spell (CL equals your character level).

### Thicket of Blades
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 61
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Any opponent you threaten that takes any movement (including a 5-foot step or the withdraw action) provokes an attack of opportunity from you before leaving its square.

### Tide of Chaos
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 61
- **Type**: Strike [Chaos]
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: One Devoted Spirit maneuver, chaotic alignment
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack. If the target is lawful-aligned, you gain a +8 bonus on the attack roll and deal an extra 4d6 damage; on a hit against a lawful target, you gain total concealment until the start of your next turn.

### Vanguard Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 62
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, all your allies gain a +4 bonus on melee and ranged attack rolls against the target until the start of your next turn.
