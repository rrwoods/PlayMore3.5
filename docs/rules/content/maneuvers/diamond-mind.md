# Diamond Mind Maneuvers and Stances

Primary Source: *Tome of Battle: The Book of Nine Swords*, Chapter 4.

### Action Before Thought
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 62
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: When required to make a Reflex save, you can make a Concentration check instead and use the result as your save. A natural 1 on the check is not an automatic failure. You must choose to use this before rolling the save.

### Avalanche of Blades
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 62
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack against a single foe. If it hits, you can make another attack against that foe at a –4 penalty. If that hits, you can make another attack at a –8 penalty, continuing with an additional cumulative –4 penalty per attack until you miss or the target drops to –1 hit points or fewer.

### Bounding Assault
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 62
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack without the straight-line movement restriction.

### Diamond Defense
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 62
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: When required to make a saving throw, you can gain a bonus on that save equal to your initiator level. You must choose to use this before rolling the save.

### Diamond Nightmare Blade
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 62
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a Concentration check against the target's AC, then make a melee attack. If the check succeeds, the attack deals quadruple normal melee damage. If the check fails, the attack takes a –2 penalty and deals normal damage.

### Disrupting Blow
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 63
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, the target must make a Will save (DC 15 + your Str modifier) or be unable to take any actions for 1 round (it can still make attacks of opportunity, but cannot take immediate actions until its next turn).

### Emerald Razor
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 63
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Diamond Mind maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack, as a touch attack.

### Hearing the Air
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 63
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You gain blindsense out to 30 feet and a +5 insight bonus on Listen checks.

### Insightful Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 63
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal damage equal to a Concentration check result instead of normal damage (Strength, weapon enhancements, and extra damage modifiers do not apply).

### Insightful Strike, Greater
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 64
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal damage equal to twice a Concentration check result instead of normal damage (Strength, weapon enhancements, and extra damage modifiers do not apply).

### Mind Over Body
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 64
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: When required to make a Fortitude save, you can make a Concentration check instead and use the result as your save. A natural 1 on the check is not an automatic failure. You must choose to use this before rolling the save.

### Mind Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 64
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, the target must make a Will save (DC 14 + your Str modifier) or take 1d4 Wisdom damage.

### Moment of Alacrity
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 64
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: At the end of this round, add 20 to your initiative count, and change your place in the turn order accordingly.

### Moment of Perfect Mind
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 64
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: When required to make a Will save, you can make a Concentration check instead and use the result as your save. A natural 1 on the check is not an automatic failure. You must choose to use this before rolling the save.

### Pearl of Black Doubt
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 64
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Diamond Mind maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Each time an opponent misses you with a melee attack, you gain a cumulative +2 dodge bonus to AC against all attacks until the start of your next turn.

### Quicksilver Motion
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 65
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Take a move action.

### Rapid Counter
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 65
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: When a foe provokes an attack of opportunity from you, you can make an immediate melee attack against that foe. This does not count against your attacks of opportunity for the round.

### Ruby Nightmare Blade
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 65
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a Concentration check against the target's AC, then make a melee attack. If the check succeeds, the attack deals double normal melee damage. If the check fails, the attack takes a –2 penalty and deals normal damage.

### Sapphire Nightmare Blade
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 65
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a Concentration check against the target's AC, then make a melee attack. If the check succeeds, the target is flat-footed against the attack and you deal an extra 1d6 damage. If the check fails, the attack takes a –2 penalty and deals normal damage.

### Stance of Alacrity
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 65
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: The first counter you initiate each round doesn't cost an immediate action.

### Stance of Clarity
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 66
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: At the start of your turn, choose a single opponent. You gain a +2 insight bonus to AC against that foe, but take a –2 penalty to AC against all other opponents.

### Time Stands Still
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 66
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 9
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Four Diamond Mind maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make two full attacks, in succession.
