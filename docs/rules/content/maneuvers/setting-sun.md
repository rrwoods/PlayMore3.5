# Setting Sun Maneuvers and Stances

Primary Source: *Tome of Battle: The Book of Nine Swords*, Chapter 4.

### Baffling Defense
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 70
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: One Setting Sun maneuver
- **Mechanics**: When an opponent attacks you, you can make a Sense Motive check and use the result instead of your AC against that attack. You must choose to use this before knowing if the attack hits.

### Ballista Throw
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 70
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee touch attack. If it hits, make a trip attempt with a +4 bonus on the ability check, without provoking attacks of opportunity or risking counter-trip. If successful, you throw the target in a 60-foot line; the target and all creatures in the line take 6d6 damage and the target lands prone at the end of the line.

### Clever Positioning
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 70
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, the target must make a Reflex save (DC 12 + your Dex modifier) or swap positions with you.

### Comet Throw
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 71
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Setting Sun maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee touch attack. If it hits, make a trip attempt with a +4 bonus on the ability check, without provoking attacks of opportunity or risking counter-trip. If successful, you throw the target up to 10 feet into an empty space (plus 5 ft. per 5 points you beat the check by); it lands prone and takes 4d6 damage. If thrown into an occupied square, the second creature also takes 4d6 damage and falls prone (Reflex save DC 14 + your Str modifier halves damage and negates knockdown), and the thrown target lands in an adjacent empty space.

### Counter Charge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 71
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: When a creature charges you, you can make an opposed Strength or Dexterity check immediately before it attacks (+4 bonus if you are larger for Str, or +4 bonus if smaller for Dex). If you win, the attack is negated and you move the creature up to 2 squares away. If you fail, the creature gains an additional +2 bonus on its charge attack.

### Devastating Throw
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 71
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Setting Sun maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Move at least 15 feet, then make a melee touch attack. If it hits, make a trip attempt with a +4 bonus on the ability check, without provoking attacks of opportunity or risking counter-trip. If successful, you throw the target up to 10 feet away (plus 5 ft. per 5 points you beat the check by); it lands prone and takes 2d6 damage.

### Feigned Opening
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 72
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Setting Sun maneuver
- **Mechanics**: When an enemy makes an attack of opportunity against you (before resolving it), you can initiate this maneuver. If the attack misses, the enemy provokes an attack of opportunity from you; if it hits, the enemy provokes an attack of opportunity from all allies who threaten her.

### Fool's Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 72
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When an enemy attacks you, you can make an opposed attack roll. If your result is higher, the enemy deals its normal attack damage to itself.

### Ghostly Defense
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 72
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Whenever an opponent's attack misses you due to a miss chance (such as concealment), you can redirect that attack to another eligible target adjacent to you using the original attack roll result. You cannot use this if flat-footed.

### Giant Killing Style
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 72
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Setting Sun maneuver
- **Mechanics**: You gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls and a +4 bonus on damage rolls against opponents of a larger size category than yours.

### Hydra Slaying Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 73
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, the target cannot make a full attack on its next turn.

### Mighty Throw
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 73
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee touch attack. If it hits, make a trip attempt with a +4 bonus on the ability check, without provoking attacks of opportunity or risking counter-trip. If successful, you throw the target up to 10 feet away into an empty space; it lands prone. Thrown foes can pass through occupied squares and do not provoke attacks of opportunity during the throw.

### Mirrored Pursuit
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 73
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When an adjacent opponent moves, you can immediately move up to your speed to any square adjacent to her as soon as she stops moving. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Scorpion Parry
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 73
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When an opponent attacks you, you can make an opposed attack roll. If your result is higher, you redirect the attack to an adjacent creature within the attacker's reach using the attacker's original attack roll.

### Shifting Defense
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 73
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Each time an opponent attacks you, you can spend an attack of opportunity to take an immediate 5-foot step. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Soaring Throw
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 73
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee touch attack. If it hits, make a trip attempt with a +4 bonus on the ability check, without provoking attacks of opportunity or risking counter-trip. If successful, you throw the target up to 20 feet away into an empty space (plus 5 ft. per 5 points you beat the check by); it lands prone and takes 8d6 damage. Thrown foes do not provoke attacks of opportunity during the throw.

### Stalking Shadow
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 74
- **Type**: Counter
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 immediate action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When an adjacent opponent moves, you can take an immediate 5-foot step into a space it just vacated. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

### Step of the Wind
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 74
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: You ignore movement and skill check penalties from difficult terrain. Against opponents in difficult terrain, you gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls and a +4 bonus on bull rush and trip checks (both to attempt and to resist).

### Strike of the Broken Shield
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 74
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 4d6 damage and the target must make a Reflex save (DC 14 + your Str modifier) or become flat-footed until the start of its next turn.

### Tornado Throw
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 75
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 9
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Five Setting Sun maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Move at least 10 feet, then make a melee touch attack. If it hits, make a trip attempt with a +4 bonus on the ability check, without provoking attacks of opportunity or risking counter-trip. If successful, you throw the target up to 10 feet away plus 5 ft. per 5 points you beat the check by. They land prone and takes 2d6 damage plus 1d6 per 5 points you beat the check by. The thrown foe does not provoke attacks of opportunity for being thrown. Repeat this process any number of times, moving a maximum total of double your speed.
