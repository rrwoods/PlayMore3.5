# Stone Dragon Maneuvers and Stances

Primary Source: *Tome of Battle: The Book of Nine Swords*, Chapter 4.

### Adamantine Bones
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 81
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you gain damage reduction 20/adamantine for 1 round.

### Ancient Mountain Hammer
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 81
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 12d6 damage and overcome damage reduction and hardness.

### Bonecrusher
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 81
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 4d6 damage and the target must make a Fortitude save (DC 13 + your Str modifier) or rolls to confirm critical hits against it gain a +10 bonus until its hit points are fully restored. (Creatures immune to critical hits are immune to this effect.)

### Bonesplitting Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 81
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal normal damage plus 2 Constitution damage.

### Boulder Roll
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 81
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: When you make an overrun attack, you gain a +4 bonus on your Strength check, do not provoke attacks of opportunity for moving or overrunning, and cannot be knocked prone if you fail.

### Charging Minotaur
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 81
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a bull rush attack as part of a charge without provoking attacks of opportunity. If your Strength check exceeds the opponent's result, you deal 2d6 + Str modifier bludgeoning damage in addition to pushing the target back (you do not need to follow).

### Colossus Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 82
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 6d6 damage and the target must make a Fortitude save (DC 17 + your Str modifier, with a –2 penalty for smaller creatures or +2 bonus for larger creatures) or be hurled 1d4 squares away from you and knocked prone without provoking attacks of opportunity.

### Crushing Vise
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 82
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 4d6 damage, and if the target is on the ground, its speed drops to 0 ft. for 1 round.

### Crushing Weight of the Mountain
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 82
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Stone Dragon maneuver
- **Mechanics**: You gain the ability to constrict for 2d6 + 1-1/2 times your Str bonus damage on a successful grapple check.

### Earthstrike Quake
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 82
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: All creatures standing on the ground within a 20-ft.-radius burst centered on you (excluding yourself) must make a Reflex save (DC 18 + your Str modifier) or fall prone; creatures actively casting a spell must succeed on a Concentration check (DC 20 + spell level) or lose the spell.

### Elder Mountain Hammer
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 82
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 6d6 damage and overcome damage reduction and hardness.

### Giant's Stance
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 83
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You deal damage with weapons and unarmed strikes as if one size category larger (maximum Large). Ends immediately if you move more than 5 feet.

### Iron Bones
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 83
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you gain damage reduction 10/adamantine for 1 round.

### Irresistible Mountain Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 83
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 4d6 damage and the target, if on the ground, must make a Fortitude save (DC 16 + your Str modifier) or be unable to take a standard action for 1 round.

### Mountain Avalanche
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 84
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You can move up to double your speed and trample creatures of your size or smaller, dealing 2d6 + 1-1/2 times your Str bonus damage once per creature when you enter all of its space. If you enter only part of a creature's space, it can make an attack of opportunity against you or a Reflex save (DC 15 + your Str modifier) for half damage.

### Mountain Hammer
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 84
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 2d6 damage and overcome damage reduction and hardness.

### Mountain Tombstone Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 84
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 9
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal 2d6 Constitution damage in addition to normal damage.

### Overwhelming Mountain Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 84
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 2d6 damage and the target, if on the ground, must make a Fortitude save (DC 14 + your Str modifier) or lose its move action for 1 round.

### Roots of the Mountain
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 84
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: You gain damage reduction 2/—, a +10 bonus on grapple checks and checks to resist bull rush, overrun, and trip, and creatures moving through your space or threatened area take a –10 penalty on Tumble checks. Ends immediately if you move more than 5 feet.

### Stone Bones
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 84
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you gain damage reduction 5/adamantine for 1 round.

### Stone Dragon's Fury
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 85
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Stone Dragon maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits a construct or object, you deal an extra 4d6 damage. Can be used in conjunction with a sunder attempt.

### Stone Vise
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 85
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 1d6 damage and the target, if on the ground, must make a Fortitude save (DC 12 + your Str modifier) or its speed drops to 0 ft. for 1 round.

### Stonefoot Stance
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 85
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: You gain a +2 bonus on Strength checks and a +2 bonus to AC against creatures larger than you. Ends immediately if you move more than 5 feet.

### Strength of Stone
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 85
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Stone Dragon maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You are immune to critical hits. Ends immediately if you move more than 5 feet.
