# Tiger Claw Maneuvers and Stances

Primary Source: *Tome of Battle: The Book of Nine Swords*, Chapter 4.

### Blood in the Water
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 86
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Tiger Claw maneuver
- **Mechanics**: You gain a cumulative +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls each time you score a critical hit; this bonus lasts until 1 minute passes without scoring a critical hit.

### Claw at the Moon
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 86
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a Jump check (DC equal to the target's AC). If successful, make a melee attack that deals an extra 2d6 damage and gains a +4 bonus on rolls to confirm critical hits; on a failure, you make a normal melee attack.

### Dancing Mongoose
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 86
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You make one additional attack with each weapon you wield (maximum two extra attacks) at your highest attack bonus against the same opponent this turn.

### Death from Above
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 86
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Tiger Claw maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a DC 20 Jump check against an adjacent foe. If successful, you land in an adjacent square within 20 ft. and make a melee attack dealing an extra 4d6 damage against a flat-footed opponent; on a failure, you remain in your space and make a normal melee attack.

### Feral Death Blow
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 87
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 9
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Four Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a Jump check against an adjacent foe (DC equal to its AC). If successful, make a melee attack against the flat-footed target; if it deals damage, the target must make a Fortitude save (DC 19 + your Str modifier) or be instantly slain (if immune to critical hits, or on a successful save, it instead takes an extra 20d6 damage). On a failed Jump check, make a normal melee attack.

### Flesh Ripper
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 87
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack against a creature vulnerable to critical hits. If it hits, the target must make a Fortitude save (DC 13 + your Str modifier) or take a –4 penalty on attacks and to AC for 1 round (or for rounds equal to your weapon's critical multiplier on a critical hit).

### Fountain of Blood
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 87
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When you reduce a living opponent in your threatened area to –1 or fewer hit points, you deal an extra 1d6 damage to it and all enemies within 30 ft. must make a Will save (DC 14 + your Str modifier, +1 per foe dropped this turn) or be shaken for 1 minute.

### Girallon Windmill Flesh Rip
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 87
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: While wielding two or more weapons, whenever you hit a creature multiple times this turn you deal rend damage based on the number of attacks that hit it: 2 hits deal 8d6, 3 hits deal 10d6, 4 hits deal 12d6, 5 hits deal 14d6, 6 hits deal 16d6, 7 hits deal 18d6, or 8+ hits deal 20d6.

### Hamstring Attack
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 88
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, the target takes normal damage, 1d8 Dexterity damage, and a –10-foot penalty to speed for 1 minute (Fortitude save DC 17 + your Str modifier halves Dex damage and speed penalty).

### Hunter's Sense
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 88
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Tiger Claw maneuver
- **Mechanics**: You gain the scent special ability (MM 314).

### Leaping Dragon Stance
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 88
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Tiger Claw maneuver
- **Mechanics**: You gain a +10-foot enhancement bonus on Jump checks, and all your jumps are treated as running jumps.

### Pouncing Charge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 88
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack, but make a full attack instead of a single attack at the end of the charge. The +2 charge bonus applies to all attack rolls.

### Prey On the Weak
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 88
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Whenever an opponent within 10 feet of you drops to –1 or fewer hit points, you can make an attack of opportunity against any opponent within your threatened area.

### Rabid Bear Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 88
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack with a +4 bonus. If it hits, you deal an extra 10d6 damage. You take a –4 penalty to AC until the start of your next turn.

### Rabid Wolf Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 88
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack with a +4 bonus. If it hits, you deal an extra 2d6 damage. You take a –4 penalty to AC until the start of your next turn.

### Raging Mongoose
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 89
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You make two additional attacks with each weapon you wield (maximum four extra attacks) at your highest attack bonus this turn.

### Soaring Raptor Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 89
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One Tiger Claw maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Against a foe of a larger size category than yours, make a Jump check (DC equal to its AC). If successful, make a melee attack with a +4 bonus dealing an extra 6d6 damage; on a failure, the maneuver is expended with no attack.

### Sudden Leap
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 89
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Tiger Claw maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a Jump check and move the distance indicated in a straight line (provokes attacks of opportunity as normal).

### Swooping Dragon Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 89
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a Jump check to jump over an opponent. If successful, you do not provoke attacks of opportunity for moving, your melee attack against the target deals an extra 10d6 damage against its flat-footed AC, and it must make a Fortitude save (DC equal to your Jump check result) or be stunned for 1 round. On a failed Jump check, you provoke attacks of opportunity and make a normal melee attack.

### Wolf Climbs the Mountain
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 89
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Against a foe of a larger size category than yours, enter its space without provoking an attack of opportunity and make a melee attack dealing an extra 5d6 damage. While you remain in its space, you gain cover against all attacks; if the target moves, it provokes an attack of opportunity from you.

### Wolf Fang Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 90
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: While wielding two weapons (or a weapon and unarmed strike), make a melee attack with each weapon against the same opponent with a –2 penalty on each attack roll.

### Wolf Pack Tactics
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 90
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two Tiger Claw maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Each time you make a successful melee attack, you can move 5 feet without provoking attacks of opportunity from that creature (up to your current speed per round).

### Wolverine Stance
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 90
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One Tiger Claw maneuver
- **Mechanics**: You can attack a grappling foe with any one-handed weapon without the –4 grapple penalty, and gain a +4 bonus on damage rolls against grappling foes of a larger size category.
