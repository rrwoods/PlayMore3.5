# White Raven Maneuvers and Stances

Primary Source: *Tome of Battle: The Book of Nine Swords*, Chapter 4.

### Battle Leader's Charge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 90
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: One White Raven maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack without provoking attacks of opportunity for moving; if it hits, you deal an extra 10 damage.

### Bolstering Voice
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 90
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Allies within 60 ft. who can hear you gain a +2 morale bonus on Will saves (+4 against fear effects).

### Clarion Call
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 91
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Three White Raven maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: When a melee attack you make during your turn reduces a foe below 0 hit points, all allies within 60 ft. who see and hear you can immediately either make one melee attack at their highest attack bonus or take a single move action.

### Covering Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 91
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One White Raven maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Any foe you hit with a melee attack this turn cannot make attacks of opportunity for 3 rounds (maximum once per encounter per target).

### Douse the Flames
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 91
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal normal damage and the target cannot make attacks of opportunity for 1 round.

### Flanking Maneuver
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 91
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Two White Raven maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack against a creature you and one or more allies are flanking. If it hits, each flanking ally who can see you can immediately make a melee attack against that creature.

### Leading the Attack
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 91
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, your allies gain a +4 morale bonus on attack rolls against that target for 1 round.

### Leading the Charge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 92
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 1
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Mechanics**: Allies within 60 ft. who can hear you gain a bonus on charge attack damage rolls equal to your initiator level.

### Lion's Roar
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 92
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One White Raven maneuver
- **Mechanics**: When you reduce an opponent below 0 hit points, you and allies within 60 ft. gain a +5 morale bonus on damage rolls for 1 round.

### Order Forged From Chaos
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 93
- **Type**: Other
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 move action
- **Prerequisites**: Two White Raven maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Allies within 30 ft. can immediately take a move action to move up to their speed (this movement provokes attacks of opportunity and does not count toward skirmish or minimum movement abilities).

### Press the Advantage
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 93
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 5
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: Two White Raven maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You can take a second 5-foot step immediately after taking your first 5-foot step for the round (or make a single 5-foot step into difficult terrain, which prevents the second step).

### Swarm Tactics
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 93
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One White Raven maneuver
- **Mechanics**: While adjacent to one or more opponents, allies within 60 ft. gain a +5 bonus on attack rolls against those opponents.

### Swarming Assault
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 93
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 7
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three White Raven maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, any ally threatening the target can immediately make a melee attack against it.

### Tactical Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 93
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 2
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One White Raven maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 2d6 damage, and each ally adjacent to the target can immediately take a 5-foot step without provoking attacks of opportunity.

### Tactics of the Wolf
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 93
- **Type**: Stance
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One White Raven maneuver
- **Mechanics**: When you flank a foe, you and allies flanking the foe with you gain a bonus on damage rolls against that opponent equal to 1/2 your initiator level.

### War Leader's Charge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 93
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 6
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Two White Raven maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a charge attack without provoking attacks of opportunity for moving; if it hits, you deal an extra 35 damage.

### War Master's Charge
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 94
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 9
- **Initiation Action**: 1 full-round action
- **Prerequisites**: Four White Raven maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: You charge an opponent without provoking attacks of opportunity for moving, dealing an extra 50 damage, and all allies within 30 ft. can also charge this target as an immediate action (dealing an extra 25 damage and without provoking attacks of opportunity). You and charging allies gain a cumulative +2 bonus on charge attack rolls per charging creature; if struck by you and at least one ally, the target is stunned for 1 round.

### White Raven Hammer
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 94
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 8
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: Three White Raven maneuvers
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 6d6 damage and the target is stunned for 1 round.

### White Raven Strike
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 94
- **Type**: Strike
- **Level**: 4
- **Initiation Action**: 1 standard action
- **Prerequisites**: One White Raven maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Make a melee attack. If it hits, you deal an extra 4d6 damage and the target is flat-footed until the start of its next turn.

### White Raven Tactics
- **Source**: *Tome of Battle*, p. 94
- **Type**: Boost
- **Level**: 3
- **Initiation Action**: 1 swift action
- **Prerequisites**: One White Raven maneuver
- **Mechanics**: Choose an ally within 10 ft. Her initiative count immediately becomes your initiative count – 1; if she has already acted this round, she can act again on her new count.
