### Whip
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 121
- **Proficiency**: Exotic | **Type**: One-Handed Melee
- **Cost**: 1 gp | **Weight**: 2 lb.
- **Damage**: 1 (T), 1d2 (S), **1d3** (M), 1d4 (L) | **Critical**: ×2 | **Damage Type**: Slashing
- **Range Increment**: —
- **Special**:
  - Deals nonlethal damage. Deals no damage to creatures with armor bonus +1 or higher or natural armor +3 or higher.
  - **Reach**: 15-ft. reach; does not threaten the area. Unlike most reach weapons, can attack foes anywhere within reach (including adjacent).
  - Attack provokes attacks of opportunity as if using a ranged weapon.
  - **Trip**: Can make trip attacks. If tripped during your own trip attempt, you can drop the whip to avoid being tripped.
  - **Disarm**: +2 bonus on opposed attack rolls to disarm an opponent (and resist disarm).

### Axe, Orc Double
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 115
- **Proficiency**: Exotic | **Type**: Two-Handed Melee
- **Cost**: 60 gp | **Weight**: 15 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d4/1d4 (T), 1d6/1d6 (S), **1d8/1d8** (M), 2d6/2d6 (L) | **Critical**: ×3 | **Damage Type**: Slashing
- **Range Increment**: —
- **Special**:

### Crossbow, Repeating Heavy
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 116
- **Proficiency**: Exotic | **Type**: Ranged
- **Cost**: 400 gp | **Weight**: 12 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d6 (T), 1d8 (S), **1d10** (M), 2d8 (L) | **Critical**: 19–20/×2 | **Damage Type**: Piercing
- **Range Increment**: 120 ft.
- **Special**:
  - Holds 5 crossbow bolts. Pulling the reloading lever is a free action.
  - Loading a new case of 5 bolts is a full-round action that provokes attacks of opportunity.
  - Must fire with two hands to use reloading lever and load a new case. Can fire one-handed or in each hand with normal heavy crossbow penalties.

### Net
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 119
- **Proficiency**: Exotic | **Type**: Ranged (thrown)
- **Cost**: 20 gp | **Weight**: 6 lb.
- **Damage**: — | **Critical**: — | **Damage Type**: —
- **Range Increment**: 10 ft.
- **Special**:
  - Ranged touch attack (max range 10 ft.); effective only against creatures within one size category of wielder.
  - **Entangled**: Target takes –2 attack penalty, –4 Dexterity penalty, half speed, and cannot charge or run.
  - Control trailing rope with opposed Strength check to limit movement. DC 15 Concentration check to cast spells while entangled.
  - Escape requires DC 20 Escape Artist check (full-round action) or bursting net with DC 25 Strength check (full-round action; 5 HP).
  - Unfolded net imposes –4 attack penalty. Folding takes 2 rounds (proficient) or 4 rounds (nonproficient).
