# Martial One-Handed Melee Weapons

Reference entries for all martial one-handed melee weapons from *Player's Handbook* Chapter 7: Equipment.

### Battleaxe
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 115
- **Proficiency**: Martial | **Type**: One-Handed Melee
- **Cost**: 10 gp | **Weight**: 6 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d4 (T), 1d6 (S), **1d8** (M), 2d6 (L) | **Critical**: ×3 | **Damage Type**: Slashing
- **Range Increment**: —
- **Special**: None

### Flail
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 117
- **Proficiency**: Martial | **Type**: One-Handed Melee
- **Cost**: 8 gp | **Weight**: 5 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d4 (T), 1d6 (S), **1d8** (M), 2d6 (L) | **Critical**: ×2 | **Damage Type**: Bludgeoning
- **Range Increment**: —
- **Special**:
  - **Disarm**: +2 bonus on opposed attack rolls to disarm an opponent (and resist disarm).
  - **Trip**: Can make trip attacks. If tripped during your own trip attempt, you can drop the flail to avoid being tripped.
