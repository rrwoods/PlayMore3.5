# Martial Ranged Weapons

Reference entries for all martial ranged weapons and ammunition from *Player's Handbook* Chapter 7: Equipment.

### Longbow
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 118
- **Proficiency**: Martial | **Type**: Ranged
- **Cost**: 75 gp | **Weight**: 3 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d4 (T), 1d6 (S), **1d8** (M), 2d6 (L) | **Critical**: ×3 | **Damage Type**: Piercing
- **Range Increment**: 100 ft.
- **Special**:
  - Requires two hands to use. Too unwieldy to use while mounted.
  - Low Strength penalty applies to damage rolls. High Strength bonus does not apply (requires composite longbow).

### Arrows (20)
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 114
- **Proficiency**: Martial | **Type**: Ammunition
- **Cost**: 1 gp | **Weight**: 3 lb.
- **Damage**: — | **Critical**: — | **Damage Type**: —
- **Range Increment**: —
- **Special**:
  - Ammunition for bows. Leather quiver holds 20 arrows.
  - Used as a melee weapon, treated as a light improvised weapon (–4 attack penalty, 1d4 M / 1d3 S damage, crit ×2).
  - Hit destroys arrow; miss has 50% chance of destruction or loss.

### Longbow, Composite
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 119
- **Proficiency**: Martial | **Type**: Ranged
- **Cost**: 100 gp | **Weight**: 3 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d4 (T), 1d6 (S), **1d8** (M), 2d6 (L) | **Critical**: ×3 | **Damage Type**: Piercing
- **Range Increment**: 110 ft.
- **Special**:
  - Requires two hands to use. Can be used while mounted.
  - Built with a Strength rating (+0 default; each +1 Str bonus rating adds 100 gp).
  - Incurs a –2 attack penalty if wielder Strength bonus is less than bow's Strength rating.
  - Adds wielder Strength bonus to damage up to the bow's rating.
  - Treated as a longbow for weapon proficiency and Weapon Focus.
