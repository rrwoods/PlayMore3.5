- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 117
- **Proficiency**: Martial | **Type**: Two-Handed Melee
- **Cost**: 15 gp | **Weight**: 10 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d6 (T), 1d8 (S), **1d10** (M), 2d8 (L) | **Critical**: 19–20/×2 | **Damage Type**: Bludgeoning
- **Range Increment**: —
- **Special**:
