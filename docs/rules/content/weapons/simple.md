# Simple Weapons

Reference entries for all simple weapons from *Player's Handbook* Chapter 7: Equipment.

### Gauntlet
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 117
- **Proficiency**: Simple | **Type**: Light Melee
- **Cost**: 2 gp | **Weight**: 1 lb.
- **Damage**: 1 (T), 1d2 (S), **1d3** (M), 1d4 (L) | **Critical**: ×2 | **Damage Type**: Bludgeoning
- **Range Increment**: —
- **Special**:
  - Deals lethal damage rather than nonlethal damage with unarmed strikes.
  - Otherwise considered an unarmed attack.
  - Cost and weight are for a single gauntlet. Medium and heavy armors (except breastplate) come with gauntlets.

### Unarmed Strike
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 121
- **Proficiency**: Simple | **Type**: Light Melee
- **Cost**: — | **Weight**: —
- **Damage**: 1 (T), 1d2 (S), **1d3** (M), 1d4 (L) | **Critical**: ×2 | **Damage Type**: Bludgeoning
- **Range Increment**: —

### Crossbow, Heavy
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 115
- **Proficiency**: Simple | **Type**: Ranged
- **Cost**: 50 gp | **Weight**: 8 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d6 (T), 1d8 (S), **1d10** (M), 2d8 (L) | **Critical**: 19–20/×2 | **Damage Type**: Piercing
- **Range Increment**: 120 ft.
- **Special**:
  - Loading is a full-round action that provokes attacks of opportunity.
  - Normally requires two hands to operate. Can shoot (but not load) with one hand at a –4 attack penalty, or shoot one in each hand as two one-handed weapons (–4 cumulative with two-weapon fighting penalties).

### Sling
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 121
- **Proficiency**: Simple | **Type**: Ranged
- **Cost**: — | **Weight**: 0 lb.
- **Damage**: 1d2 (T), 1d3 (S), **1d4** (M), 1d6 (L) | **Critical**: ×2 | **Damage Type**: Bludgeoning
- **Range Increment**: 50 ft.
