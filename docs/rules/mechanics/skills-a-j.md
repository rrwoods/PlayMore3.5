# Skills: A through J

## 1. Skill Reference Entries (A–J)

### Appraise
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 67
- **Key Ability**: Int | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1 minute (10 consecutive full-round actions) | **Retry**: No
- **Synergy**: Craft (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Appraise checks for related items.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Untrained checks can appraise common items; evaluating rare or exotic items requires training or DM permission.
  - Success yields accurate market value; failure by 4 or less estimates value within 50% to 150%; failure by 5 or more yields wild misdirection (secret DM roll).
  - Does not determine whether an item possesses magical properties.

### Balance
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 67
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: Yes
- **Action**: Part of movement | **Retry**: None
- **Synergy**: Tumble (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Balance checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Move at half speed while balancing. Can move at full speed by taking a -5 penalty on the check.
  - Flat-Footed Condition: A balancing character is flat-footed and loses Dexterity bonus to AC (if any) unless possessing 5 or more ranks in Balance.
  - Taking Damage: If a character takes damage while balancing, they must immediately succeed on a Balance check or fall.
  - Failure by 4 or less halts movement for the round; failure by 5 or more results in a fall.

### Bluff
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 67
- **Key Ability**: Cha | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Interaction (at least 1 round), or standard action (combat feint) | **Retry**: Varies
- **Synergy**: Bluff (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Diplomacy, Intimidate, and Sleight of Hand checks, and on Disguise checks when acting in character.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Opposed by target's Sense Motive check.
  - Feint in Combat: Standard action (or move action with Improved Feint feat). Check opposed by target's Sense Motive check (target adds base attack bonus or Sense Motive modifier, whichever is higher). Success denies the target their Dexterity bonus to AC against the character's next melee attack on or before their next turn.
  - Create Diversion to Hide: Standard action. Opposed by Sense Motive. Success allows an immediate Hide check.
  - Deliver Secret Message: Full-round action to convey hidden communication to allies.

### Climb
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 69
- **Key Ability**: Str | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: Yes
- **Action**: Part of movement (move action) | **Retry**: None
- **Synergy**: Use Rope (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Climb checks involving ropes.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Movement Speed: Climbing movement is one-quarter character speed as a move action, or one-half speed with a -5 penalty on the check.
  - Flat-Footed and Shield: Any climber without a natural climb speed loses Dexterity bonus to AC and cannot use a shield while climbing.
  - Taking Damage: Taking damage while climbing requires an immediate Climb check or the character falls.
  - Catching Falling Character: Reaction/free action to catch oneself or a falling ally on a wall or slope.
  - Natural Climb Speed: Grants a +8 racial bonus on Climb checks, retains Dexterity bonus to AC, and allows taking 10 even if rushed or threatened.

### Concentration
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 69
- **Key Ability**: Con | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Free action / Reaction | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Required to cast a spell, concentrate on an active spell, use a spell-like ability, or direct an ability while distracted, damaged, entangled, grappling, or subjected to weather or motion.
  - Casting Defensively: Standard casting action made without provoking attacks of opportunity. Failure loses the spell without effect.
  - Interruption by Damage: Taking damage during casting or concentration requires a check ($\text{DC} = 10 + \text{damage dealt} + \text{spell level}$; continuous damage: $\text{DC} = 10 + \frac{1}{2}\text{ damage} + \text{spell level}$).

### Craft
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 70
- **Key Ability**: Int | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Downtime action (by day or week) | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: Craft (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on related Appraise checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Tracked as separate skills for each distinct discipline (e.g., Craft [armorsmithing], Craft [bowmaking], Craft [weaponsmithing]). Craft (alchemy) is trained-only and requires an alchemist's lab.
  - Raw Materials Cost: One-third of finished item's market price in gold pieces.
  - Weekly Progress: $\text{Progress (sp)} = \text{Check Result} \times \text{Craft DC}$. Item is completed when accumulated silver pieces equals item price in silver pieces.
  - Daily Progress: $\text{Progress (cp)} = \text{Check Result} \times \text{Craft DC}$ in copper pieces per day.
  - Material Loss on Failure: Failure by 4 or less results in no progress; failure by 5 or more ruins half the raw materials.
  - Masterwork Component: Crafted as a separate component with a market price of 300 gp (3,000 sp) at DC 20.
  - Tools: Requires artisan's tools; working without tools incurs a -2 circumstance penalty.

### Decipher Script
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 71
- **Key Ability**: Int | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1 minute (10 consecutive full-round actions) per page | **Retry**: No
- **Synergy**: Decipher Script (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Use Magic Device checks involving scrolls.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Used to read archaic, foreign, incomplete, or coded messages.
  - Success deciphers the rough meaning of one page of text (or equivalent).
  - Failure by 5 or more draws an erroneous conclusion (secret DM check).

### Diplomacy
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 71
- **Key Ability**: Cha | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1 full minute (10 consecutive full-round actions); rushed attempt is 1 full-round action at -10 penalty | **Retry**: Generally ineffective
- **Synergy**: Bluff (5+ ranks), Knowledge (nobility and royalty) (5+ ranks), and Sense Motive (5+ ranks) each grant a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks (stacks up to +6).
- **Mechanics**:
  - Shifts NPC attitudes across five categories: Hostile, Unfriendly, Indifferent, Friendly, and Helpful.
  - Rushed Check: Can attempt to change attitude as a 1 full-round action at a -10 penalty.
  - Ineffective against creatures with Intelligence less than 3 or creatures unable to understand the speaker.

### Disable Device
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 72
- **Key Ability**: Int | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1 round (simple) to 2d4 rounds (complex) | **Retry**: Yes (if failed by 4 or less)
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Used to disarm traps, jam locks, or sabotage mechanisms.
  - Magical Traps: Only rogues (and characters with the trapfinding class feature) can disarm magical traps.
  - Tools: Requires thieves' tools. Attempting without tools incurs a -2 circumstance penalty.
  - Consequence of Failure: Failure by 5 or more immediately triggers the trap or mechanism.

### Disguise
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 72
- **Key Ability**: Cha | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1d3 $\times$ 10 minutes | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: Bluff (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Disguise checks when acting in character.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Disguise check is opposed by Spot check of observers.
  - Tools: Requires a disguise kit. Attempting without a kit incurs a -2 circumstance penalty.
  - Categorical Modifiers: Check modifier is adjusted for changing sex, changing race, or changing age category.

### Escape Artist
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 73
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: Yes
- **Action**: 1 minute (ropes/manacles/tight space), standard (grapple/pin, per *Player's Handbook* p. 156), full-round action (entangling magic/net) | **Retry**: Varies (Yes for grapple/manacles/tight space; No for rope bindings)
- **Synergy**: Escape Artist (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Use Rope checks to bind someone. Use Rope (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Escape Artist checks involving ropes.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Escaping Ropes: Opposed by binder's Use Rope check result + 10. Once failed against rope bindings, cannot retry unless bonds are loosened.
  - Escaping Grapple or Pin: Opposed by opponent's grapple check result. Success frees character from pin or grapple.
  - Squeezing: Takes at least 1 minute to squeeze through a tight space where head fits but shoulders do not.
  - Entangling Effects: Escaping from a net, *animate rope*, *entangle*, *command plants*, or *control plants* is a full-round action (DC 20 for net).

### Forgery
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 74
- **Key Ability**: Int | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1d4 minutes (short document) to 1d4 $\times$ 10 minutes (complex document) | **Retry**: No
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Creates a false document or counterfeit signature.
  - Opposed by reader's Forgery check. Reader only rolls if having a specific reason to suspect forgery.
  - Requires writing materials; unfamiliar language requires literacy in that language.

### Gather Information
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 74
- **Key Ability**: Cha | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1d4 + 1 hours | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: Knowledge (local) (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Gather Information checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Canvasses an area to uncover general rumors, find informants, or investigate specific gossip.
  - Standard expense: 1d10 gold pieces for bribes, drinks, and incidentals.

### Handle Animal
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 74
- **Key Ability**: Cha | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Handling: move action; Pushing: full-round action; Training: weeks or months | **Retry**: Yes (except rearing)
- **Synergy**: Handle Animal (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Ride checks and wild empathy checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Handling an Animal: Move action to command animal to perform a known trick.
  - Pushing an Animal: Full-round action to force animal to perform a trick it does not know or while injured.
  - Teaching Tricks: 1 to 2 weeks per trick. Animal can learn up to 3 tricks per point of Intelligence (maximum 6 for Int 2).
  - Training for General Purpose: Several weeks to train animal for combat, guard, hunting, or labor.
  - Rearing: 1 year of care to domesticate a wild animal.
  - Handling wild or magical beasts with Int 1 or 2 incurs a -5 penalty.

### Heal
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 75
- **Key Ability**: Wis | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Standard action (first aid, treat wound/poison), 10 minutes (treat disease), 8 hours light activity (long-term care) | **Retry**: Varies
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - First Aid: Standard action (DC 15). Stabilizes dying character at -1 to -9 HP, halting 1 HP loss per round. Can retry if subject is still alive.
  - Long-Term Care: DC 15. Providing 8 hours of light activity allows up to 6 patients to recover hit points and ability points at double normal rate (triple for 24-hour complete bed rest). Caregiver cannot give long-term care to self.
  - Treat Wound: Standard action (DC 15). Cleans and dresses caltrop, *spike growth*, or *spike stones* wound, restoring movement speed to normal.
  - Treat Poison: Standard action. Check replaces patient's saving throw if check result is higher.
  - Treat Disease: 10 minutes. Check replaces patient's saving throw against disease if check result is higher.
  - Equipment: A healer's kit (10 uses) grants a +2 circumstance bonus on Heal checks (not required to perform checks).

### Hide
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 76
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: Yes
- **Action**: Usually none (part of movement) | **Retry**: None
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Opposed by Spot check of observers.
  - Prerequisite: Character must have cover or concealment to make a Hide check. If observed, cannot hide unless using a Bluff diversion or possessing Hide in Plain Sight.
  - Movement Penalties: Moving faster than half speed incurs a -5 penalty; running or charging incurs a -20 penalty.
  - Sniping: Attacking from a hidden position at least 10 ft away allows an immediate move action Hide check at a -20 penalty.
  - Size Modifiers: Colossal -16, Gargantuan -12, Huge -8, Large -4, Medium +0, Small +4, Tiny +8, Diminutive +12, Fine +16.

### Intimidate
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 76
- **Key Ability**: Cha | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1 minute (social); standard action (demoralize in combat) | **Retry**: Optional
- **Synergy**: Bluff (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Intimidate checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Social Influence: 1 minute. Opposed by target's check ($1\text{d}20 + \text{Character Level or HD} + \text{Wis mod} + \text{Fear Save Modifiers}$). Success renders target friendly for 1d6 $\times$ 10 minutes.
  - Demoralize in Combat: Standard action within 30 feet. Opposed by target's check ($1\text{d}20 + \text{Character Level or HD} + \text{Wis mod} + \text{Fear Save Modifiers}$). Success renders target shaken for 1 round.
  - Size Modifier: $+4$ bonus per size category larger than target; $-4$ penalty per size category smaller.

### Jump
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 77
- **Key Ability**: Str | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: Yes
- **Action**: Part of movement | **Retry**: None
- **Synergy**: Tumble (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Jump checks. Jump (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Tumble checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Speed Modifier: Base speed 30 ft has no modifier. Speeds below 30 ft incur a -6 penalty per 10 ft below 30 ft. Speeds above 30 ft grant a +4 bonus per 10 ft above 30 ft.
  - Running Start: Requires 20 ft of straight-line movement before jump. Without a running start, check DC is doubled.
  - Movement Cap: Distance moved by jumping counts against maximum movement allowed in the round.
  - Vertical Reach: Maximum reach equals jump height plus character height (or $1.5\times$ height for long arms).
  - Jumping Down: Intentional jump down reduces effective falling distance by 10 feet.
