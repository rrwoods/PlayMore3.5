# Skills: K through Z

## 1. Skill Reference Entries (K–Z)

### Knowledge
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 78
- **Key Ability**: Int | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Usually none | **Retry**: No
- **Synergy**: 5+ ranks in individual disciplines grant +2 bonuses: Arcana $\to$ Spellcraft; Arch & Eng $\to$ Search (secret doors); Dungeoneering $\to$ Survival (underground); Geography $\to$ Survival (hazards/lost); History $\to$ Bardic Knowledge; Local $\to$ Gather Information; Nature $\to$ Survival (aboveground); Nobility & Royalty $\to$ Diplomacy; Religion $\to$ Turn/Rebuke Undead; The Planes $\to$ Survival (other planes). Survival (5+ ranks) grants +2 on Knowledge (nature).
- **Mechanics**:
  - Tracked as 10 distinct disciplines: Arcana, Architecture and Engineering, Dungeoneering, Geography, History, Local, Nature, Nobility and Royalty, Religion, The Planes.
  - Untrained Checks: Untrained characters cannot answer questions with a DC higher than 10.
  - Monster Identification: Identifies monster types, traits, and vulnerabilities ($\text{DC} = 10 + \text{monster HD}$ for common monsters; $15 + \text{HD}$ for rare).

### Listen
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 78
- **Key Ability**: Wis | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Reactive (none) or move action (active listening) | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Opposed by Move Silently check of sneaking creatures.
  - Sleeping Characters: Sleeping characters take a -10 penalty on Listen checks.
  - Distance Modifier: $-1$ penalty per 10 feet of distance to sound source.
  - Obstructions: Sound through doors, walls, and barriers incurs circumstance penalties.

### Move Silently
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 79
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: Yes
- **Action**: Part of movement | **Retry**: None
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Opposed by Listen check of observers.
  - Movement Penalties: Moving faster than normal half speed incurs a -5 penalty; running or charging incurs a -20 penalty.
  - Surface conditions (e.g. noisy materials, dry leaves) impose circumstance penalties.

### Open Lock
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 79
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Full-round action | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Tools: Requires thieves' tools; operating without tools is impossible or requires improvised tools (-2 circumstance penalty).
  - Taking 20: Character may take 20 on Open Lock checks unless the lock is trapped.

### Perform
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 79
- **Key Ability**: Cha | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Standard action (bardic music), evening (public performance) | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Tracked independently across nine distinct categories: Act, Comedy, Dance, Keyboard instruments, Oratory, Percussion instruments, String instruments, Wind instruments, Sing.
  - Bardic Music: Bard class features require specific minimum ranks in Perform to activate abilities (e.g., Countersong, Fascinate, Inspire Courage, Inspire Competence, Suggestion, Inspire Greatness, Song of Freedom, Inspire Heroics).

### Profession
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 80
- **Key Ability**: Wis | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Downtime action (weekly) | **Retry**: Varies
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Represents specialized vocations and livelihoods (e.g. sailor, merchant, miner, farmer, scribe).
  - Untrained characters cannot practice a profession.
  - Weekly Income: A character earns half their Profession check result in gold pieces per week of dedicated work.

### Ride
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 80
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Varies (Mounting or dismounting is a move action; other checks are move action, free action, or no action) | **Retry**: None
- **Synergy**: Handle Animal (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Ride checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Guide with Knees: Free action (DC 5) to steer mount, leaving both hands free to fight.
  - Stay in Saddle: No action (DC 5) when taking damage while mounted or mount rearing.
  - Fight with Warhorse: Free action (DC 10) to direct a combat-trained mount to attack.
  - Cover: No action (DC 15) to drop to far side of mount, gaining cover (+4 AC, +2 Reflex saves); cannot attack or cast spells while using mount as cover.
  - Soft Fall: No action (DC 15) when unseated or mount falls, avoiding 1d6 falling damage.
  - Fast Mount/Dismount: Free action (DC 20, takes move action on failure). Armor check penalty applies to this check.
  - Bareback Penalty: Riding without a saddle incurs a -5 penalty on Ride checks. Military saddle grants a +2 circumstance bonus to stay in the saddle.

### Search
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 81
- **Key Ability**: Int | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Full-round action per 5-foot-by-5-foot area or object | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: Search (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Survival checks to find or follow tracks. Knowledge (architecture and engineering) (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Search checks for secret doors and compartments.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Used to find concealed objects, secret doors, hidden compartments, or traps within 10 feet.
  - Traps: Any character can find mundane traps with DC 20 or lower. Only rogues (and characters with the trapfinding class feature) can find traps with DC higher than 20 or magic traps.

### Sense Motive
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 81
- **Key Ability**: Wis | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: 1 minute of interaction, or reaction (opposed check) | **Retry**: No
- **Synergy**: Sense Motive (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Opposes Bluff checks (feints in combat, false pretenses, secret messages).
  - Hunch: Assessing a social situation or gut feeling.
  - Detect Enchantment: 1 minute of observation to determine if another creature is under an enchantment effect (DC 25, or DC 15 if target is dominated).

### Sleight of Hand
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 81
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: Yes
- **Action**: Standard action (or free action with -20 penalty) | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: Bluff (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Sleight of Hand checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Used to palm objects, pick pockets, or conceal light weapons on the body.
  - Opposed by Spot check of onlookers.
  - Concealed Weapon: Small weapons or daggers grant a +2 circumstance bonus on check to conceal.
  - Free Action: Can make a Sleight of Hand check as a free action by taking a -20 penalty on the check.

### Speak Language
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 82
- **Key Ability**: None | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Not applicable | **Retry**: Not applicable
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Does not utilize ranks or rolls; languages are either known or unknown.
  - Cost: 1 skill point buys 1 language as a class skill; 2 skill points buy 1 language as a cross-class skill.
  - Literacy: Knowing a language confers full speaking, reading, and writing capability (except 1st-level barbarians, who are illiterate until spending 2 skill points).
  - Bonus Languages: 1st-level bonus languages are selected from racial and Intelligence bonus allowances.
  - Secret Languages: Secret languages (e.g. Druidic) cannot be selected without class eligibility.

### Spellcraft
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 82
- **Key Ability**: Int | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: No action / free action, or standard action / 8 hours depending on task | **Retry**: Varies
- **Synergy**: Knowledge (arcana) (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Spellcraft checks. Spellcraft (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Use Magic Device checks involving scrolls. Use Magic Device (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Spellcraft checks to decipher scrolls.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Identify Spell Being Cast: No action required; requires seeing or hearing spell components (DC 15 + spell level).
  - Identify Spell in Place: Requires *detect magic* and 1 round of study (DC 20 + spell level).
  - Learn Spell into Spellbook: 8 hours of study per spell (DC 25, wizard only).
  - Prepare Spells from Borrowed Spellbook: Requires daily check to prepare spells (DC 15 + spell level).
  - Decipher Scroll Script: Full-round action without *read magic* (DC 20 + spell level).

### Spot
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 83
- **Key Ability**: Wis | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Reactive (none) or move action (active scanning) | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Opposes Hide checks of stealthy creatures, and Disguise or Sleight of Hand checks.
  - Distance Modifier: $-1$ penalty per 10 feet of distance to observed creature or object.
  - Lighting and concealment modifiers apply.

### Survival
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 83
- **Key Ability**: Wis | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Varies (24 hours for foraging; part of movement for tracking) | **Retry**: Varies
- **Synergy**: Knowledge (nature) (5+ ranks), Knowledge (dungeoneering) (5+ ranks), Knowledge (geography) (5+ ranks), and Knowledge (the planes) (5+ ranks) grant situational +2 bonuses. Search (5+ ranks) grants +2 on Survival checks to follow tracks. Survival (5+ ranks) grants +2 on Knowledge (nature).
- **Mechanics**:
  - Foraging: Sustains character and 1 additional person per 2 points check exceeds DC.
  - Wayfinding: Prevents becoming lost and avoids natural environmental hazards.
  - Weather Forecasting: Predicts weather 24 hours in advance.
  - Tracking: Requires the Track feat to follow tracks with DC higher than 10. Moves at half speed without penalty, full speed at -5 penalty, or double speed at -20 penalty.

### Swim
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 84
- **Key Ability**: Str | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: Double normal penalty
- **Action**: Move action (one-quarter speed) or full-round action (one-half speed) | **Retry**: None
- **Synergy**: None
- **Mechanics**:
  - Double normal armor check penalty applies to Swim checks (-2 per point of armor check penalty).
  - Success allows one-quarter speed movement (move action) or one-half speed movement (full-round action).
  - Failure by 4 or less means no progress and treading water; failure by 5 or more causes character to submerge and begin drowning.
  - Holding Breath: Can hold breath for rounds equal to $2 \times \text{Constitution score}$ (or $1 \times \text{Constitution}$ if taking standard or full-round actions). Then requires Constitution checks each round (DC 10, +1 per previous round) or falls unconscious and drowns.
  - Natural Swim Speed: Grants a +8 racial bonus on Swim checks, allows always taking 10, and permits using the run action while swimming.

### Tumble
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 84
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: Yes
- **Action**: Part of movement | **Retry**: No
- **Synergy**: Tumble (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Balance and Jump checks. Jump (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Tumble checks.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Avoid Attacks of Opportunity: Move at half speed through threatened squares without provoking attacks of opportunity.
  - Move Through Enemy Space: Move at half speed through an opponent's occupied square without provoking attacks of opportunity.
  - Accelerated Tumbling: Move at full speed while tumbling by accepting a -10 penalty on the check.
  - Break Fall: No action (DC 15 when falling); success treats the fall as 10 feet shorter.
  - Armor Restriction: Cannot tumble while wearing medium or heavy armor or carrying a medium or heavy load.
  - Combat Defense Boost: 5 or more ranks in Tumble increases dodge bonus from fighting defensively from +2 to +3 dodge bonus to AC, and increases dodge bonus from total defense from +4 to +6 dodge bonus to AC.

### Use Magic Device
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 85
- **Key Ability**: Cha | **Trained Only**: Yes | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Same as device activation | **Retry**: Yes (except on natural 1 lockout)
- **Synergy**: Decipher Script (5+ ranks) and Spellcraft (5+ ranks) each grant a +2 bonus on UMD checks involving scrolls. UMD (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Spellcraft checks to decipher scrolls.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Taking 10 & Aid Another Prohibited: A character cannot take 10 on Use Magic Device checks, and cannot aid another on Use Magic Device checks.
  - Device Activation: Allows activating magic items requiring class spell lists, alignment, class features, or race.
  - Emulate Ability Score: Effective score equals check result minus 15.
  - Emulate Class Feature: Effective class level equals check result minus 20.
  - Blind Activation: DC 25 to activate blindly; character gains a +2 bonus on the check if having activated the item previously. Failure by 10 or more triggers a mishap (2d6 damage or targeting error).
  - Natural 1 Mishap: Rolling a natural 1 on a failed check locks the character out of activating that specific item for 24 hours.

### Use Rope
- **Source**: *Player's Handbook*, p. 86
- **Key Ability**: Dex | **Trained Only**: No | **Armor Check Penalty**: No
- **Action**: Standard action (knots, grappling hook), 1 minute (bindings), 5 minutes (splicing) | **Retry**: Yes
- **Synergy**: Use Rope (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Climb checks involving ropes and on Escape Artist checks involving ropes. Escape Artist (5+ ranks) grants a +2 bonus on Use Rope checks to bind someone.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Tie Knots and Slipknots: Standard action (DC 10 for firm knot, DC 15 for special/slipknot).
  - Bind a Prisoner: 1 minute. Check result + 10 becomes the DC for the prisoner's Escape Artist check to slip bonds.
  - Grappling Hook: Standard action to secure a grappling hook (DC 10, +2 per 10 ft thrown). Failure by 5 or more means the hook slips when weight is applied.
  - Splice Ropes: 5 minutes of work (DC 15).
