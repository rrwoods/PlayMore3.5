# Weapons

Systemic combat rules, weapon categories, size scaling, and master weapon tables from *Player's Handbook* Chapter 7: Equipment (pp. 112–122) and Chapter 8: Combat (p. 134).

---

## 1. Weapon Categories & Proficiency

- **Proficiency Categories**:
  - **Simple Weapons**: Basic weapons requiring minimal specialized combat training.
  - **Martial Weapons**: Standard military weapons requiring specialized combat training.
  - **Exotic Weapons**: Specialized, unusual, or culturally distinct weapons requiring training in each specific weapon.
  - **Nonproficiency Penalty**: A character using a weapon without proficiency takes a **-4 penalty** on attack rolls.
- **Combat Roles**:
  - **Melee Weapons**: Used for melee attacks; some can also be thrown.
  - **Ranged Weapons**: Thrown weapons or projectile weapons ineffective in melee.
  - **Reach Weapons**: Strike non-adjacent targets at double natural reach (typically 10 ft. for Small/Medium, unable to strike adjacent 5 ft.; 15–20 ft. for Large, unable to strike 5–10 ft.).
  - **Double Weapons**: Can be used as two weapons (incurring standard Two-Weapon Fighting penalties as a one-handed weapon and a light off-hand weapon), or wielded two-handed attacking with one end. When wielded one-handed, only one end can be used per round.
  - **Thrown Weapons**: Add Strength modifier to damage (except splash weapons). Maximum range is 5 range increments. Throwing a melee weapon not designed for throwing incurs a -4 attack penalty, is a standard action (light/one-handed) or full-round action (two-handed), has a 10 ft. increment, and threatens critical only on natural 20 (×2).
  - **Projectile Weapons**: Add no Strength bonus to damage unless composite bow or sling. Strength penalties apply to all bow and sling damage rolls. Maximum range is 10 range increments.
  - **Ammunition**: Drawing bow ammunition is a free action; crossbows and slings require reload actions. Ammunition that hits is destroyed; misses have a 50% chance of loss or destruction. Shuriken are treated as ammunition for drawing, crafting, and recovery.

## 2. Weapon Encumbrance & Handling

- **Light Weapons**: Usable in one hand and while grappling. Add Strength bonus in primary hand, or 1/2 Strength bonus in off-hand. Two-handed wielding provides no additional damage bonus (*PHB* p. 113, p. 134). Unarmed strikes are treated as light weapons.
- **One-Handed Weapons**: Usable in primary or off-hand (1× or 0.5× Str bonus). When wielded with two hands in melee combat, add **1-1/2 times** Strength bonus to damage rolls (*PHB* p. 113, p. 134).
- **Two-Handed Weapons**: Require two hands. Add **1-1/2 times** Strength bonus to melee damage rolls (*PHB* p. 113, p. 134).
- A one-handed weapon wielded in two hands is treated as a two-handed weapon for mechanics and effects applying to two-handed weapons (implied by *Player's Handbook*, p. 98, and p. 113).

## 3. Weapon Size & Inappropriately Sized Weapons

- **Size Keying**: Weapon size indicates intended wielder size, not object size.
  - Light weapon: Object two size categories smaller than wielder.
  - One-handed weapon: Object one size category smaller than wielder.
  - Two-handed weapon: Object same size category as wielder.
- **Attack Penalty**: Cumulative **-2 penalty** on attack rolls for each size category difference between weapon and actual wielder.
- **Effort Step Shift**: Handling effort shifts by one step per size category difference (e.g. Small greatsword is one-handed for Medium creature; Large dagger is one-handed for Medium creature). If effort shifts beyond light, one-handed, or two-handed, the weapon cannot be wielded.
- **Cost & Weight Scaling**:
  - Small and Medium: Listed cost.
  - Large: 2× listed cost.
  - Small weight: 0.5× listed weight.
  - Medium weight: 1× listed weight.
  - Large weight: 2× listed weight.

## 4. Damage, Critical Hits, & Damage Types

- **Critical Hits**: On a threat and confirmation hit, roll damage N times as indicated by critical multiplier (with all static modifiers). Precision damage (sneak attack) and weapon special energy dice are not multiplied.
- **Damage Types**:
  - **Bludgeoning (B)**, **Piercing (P)**, **Slashing (S)**.
  - **Multiple Types ("and")**: The weapon deals both types simultaneously; damage is not split. Damage reduction or immunity applies only if the target resists both types (e.g. morningstar deals bludgeoning and piercing).
  - **Multiple Types ("or")**: Wielder selects which type to deal at attack time (e.g. dagger deals piercing or slashing).
