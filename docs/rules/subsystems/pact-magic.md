# Pact Magic

## Pact Magic Overview and Soul Binding Ritual (Drawing the Seal, Summoning, Making the Pact)
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 7–10, 17–19
- **Core Concept**: Pact magic allows a binder to contact and bargain with vestiges—remnants of once-living entities (mortal souls, slain deities, banished outsiders) that exist outside life, death, and undeath beyond the reach of normal reality and divine power.
- **Drawing the Seal**:
  - Scribing: The binder must visibly draw the vestige's unique geometric seal on a relatively flat surface (typically the ground) using chalk, charcoal, or similar material.
  - Size: The seal must be at least 5 feet across.
  - Time & Concentration: Drawing requires 1 minute (10 consecutive rounds) of concentration and provokes attacks of opportunity.
  - Potency: A seal not used within 1 minute of completion loses all potency and must be redrawn.
  - Special Requirements: Any vestige-specific requirement (location, items, attributes) must be satisfied for the seal to function.
- **Summoning Ritual**:
  - Time & Action: Requires a full-round action within 1 minute of drawing the seal (*Tome of Magic*, p. 10).
  - Vocalization: The binder must touch the seal and call out the vestige's name and title. The ritual automatically fails if the binder cannot be heard (such as in the area of a *silence* spell).
  - Manifestation: A manifestation of the vestige appears in the seal's space. The image that appears is explicitly a "supernatural figment" (*Tome of Magic*, p. 19)—an illusion that cannot harm or be harmed (*Tome of Magic*, p. 10), cannot be dispelled, vanishes in an *antimagic field* (*Tome of Magic*, p. 19), and ignores all creatures except the binder (*Tome of Magic*, p. 10).
  - Expiration: If the binder fails to address the manifestation within 1 round, it disappears. The vestige communicates in whatever language was used to summon it.
- **Making the Pact**:
  - The binder formally addresses the summoned manifestation and requests a 24-hour pact.
  - The contest of wills is resolved by making a **Binding Check**.
  - Rushed Binding: A binder can rush the process as a full-round action at a -10 penalty on the binding check instead of taking 1 minute.
  - Solitary: The pact must be made alone; others cannot aid the binder in any way (no Aid Another).
  - Binding is Inevitable: Whether the check succeeds or fails, the pact is formed, the vestige vanishes, the binder gains all granted abilities for 24 hours, and the physical sign manifests.

## Effective Binder Level (EBL)
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 10, 11, 19
- **Definition**: Effective Binder Level (EBL) equals the character's Binder class level plus any soul binding bonuses granted by prestige classes (e.g., Anima Mage, Knight of the Sacred Seal) or other sources (*Tome of Magic*, p. 10).
- **Scope & Functions**: EBL governs:
  - Maximum vestige level the character can summon (*Tome of Magic*, p. 10).
  - Total number of vestiges that can be bound simultaneously (*Tome of Magic*, p. 10).
  - Caster level for vestige-granted abilities duplicating spells or mysteries (CL = EBL) (*Tome of Magic*, p. 19).
  - Difficulty Class for vestige supernatural abilities ($\text{DC} = 10 + \left\lfloor\frac{1}{2}\text{EBL}\right\rfloor + \text{CHA mod}$) (*Tome of Magic*, p. 11).
  - Bonus on binding checks ($1\text{d}20 + \text{EBL} + \text{CHA mod}$) (*Tome of Magic*, p. 10).
  - Scaling numerical effects of specific vestige powers (damage dice, range, durations).
- **Modifiers & Exceptions**:
  - *Improved Binding Feat* (*Tome of Magic*, p. 74): Increases EBL by +2 solely for determining the maximum level of vestige the character can bind. Does not increase EBL for any other calculation.
  - *Favored Vestige Feat* (*Tome of Magic*, p. 74): Increases EBL by +1 when using the abilities granted by the chosen vestige.
  - *Bind Vestige Feat* (*Tome of Magic*, p. 72): Characters without the soul binding class feature treat their EBL as 1st level (or 5th level with *Improved Bind Vestige*), binding only one vestige and gaining only one assigned power.

## Binding Check (1d20 + Effective Binder Level + CHA Modifier vs. Vestige Binding DC)
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 10, 17, 19
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Binding Check} = 1\text{d}20 + \text{Effective Binder Level} + \text{Charisma modifier}$$
- **Check Difficulty (DC)**: Each vestige possesses an intrinsic Binding DC listed in its summary (e.g., DC 15 for 1st-level Aym up to DC 35 for 8th-level Orthos; see Table 1–2).
- **Duration**: Takes 1 minute of concentration, or a full-round action if rushed at a -10 penalty.
- **Unaided**: Must be rolled alone without assistance from other creatures.
- **Function**: The check does not prevent binding; it strictly determines whether a **Good Pact** or **Poor Pact** is established for the 24-hour duration.

## Good Pact vs. Poor Pact
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 10–11
- **Good Pact (Check $\ge$ DC)**:
  - The vestige has no control over the binder's actions and does not influence their personality.
  - Once reaching Binder level 2+ via Suppress Sign (*Tome of Magic*, p. 11), the binder can suppress the physical sign of the vestige at will as a swift action.
- **Poor Pact (Check $<$ DC)**:
  - The vestige influences the binder's personality, demeanor, and behavioral impulses for the duration of the pact.
  - The binder cannot suppress the vestige's physical sign (even with the *Suppress Sign* class feature) and must bear it openly for the full 24 hours.
  - The binder must adhere to the vestige's behavioral requirements or incur cumulative defiance penalties.

## Vestige Sign (Showing vs. Suppressing)
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 10–11, 20
- **Physical Manifestation**: Binding merges a shard of the vestige's spirit with the binder's soul, causing a distinctive physical sign to appear on the binder's body (e.g., ram horns, stars in eyes, blood weeping, sulfuric smell).
- **Not Illusory**: The sign is real physical transmutation, not an illusion or shapechange; creatures viewing the binder with *true seeing* see the sign as it actually appears.
- **Supernatural Status**: The sign is explicitly a supernatural effect: "The sign is a supernatural effect and therefore is suppressed when the binding is suppressed" (*Tome of Magic*, p. 20), such as within an *antimagic field*.
- **Concealment**: The binder may conceal the sign using mundane coverings (cloaks, bandages, hats) or magical disguise (*disguise self*) without penalty.
- **Suppress Sign (Ex)**: Gained at Binder 2nd level. When a **Good Pact** is formed, the binder can suppress or reveal the sign at will as a **swift action**. On a **Poor Pact**, the sign cannot be suppressed.

## Vestige Influence and Defiance Penalty (Cumulative -1 Penalty per Defiance)
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 10, 19, 20
- **Behavioral Influence**: Under a poor pact, the vestige exerts psychological pressure on the binder, altering their demeanor and requiring them to perform or refrain from specific actions described in the vestige's entry.
- **Defiance Trigger**: If a conscious, free-willed binder encounters a situation where they cannot or will not perform a required action or refrain from a prohibited action, they defy the vestige.
- **Defiance Penalty**: The binder incurs a **-1 penalty on attack rolls, saving throws, and checks**.
- **Duration**: The penalty persists until that vestige leaves the binder—normally at the end of the 24-hour pact, or earlier if it is expelled with Expel Vestige (*Tome of Magic*, pp. 10, 73).
- **Stacking**: Penalties are cumulative and stack:
  - Disobeying the same vestige multiple times adds an additional -1 penalty per violation.
  - Defying multiple bound vestiges causes the penalties from each vestige to stack together.

## Pact Duration (24 Hours) and Expelling a Vestige
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 10, 73
- **Duration**: Exactly **24 hours** from completion of the summoning and binding ritual.
- **Irrevocable**: Neither the binder nor the vestige can choose to sever the pact prematurely under normal circumstances. The bond cannot be dispelled by *dispel magic* or removed by exorcism.
- **Antimagic Suppression**: An *antimagic field* suppresses the vestige's granted powers and sign for as long as the binder remains in the area, but does not break or pause the 24-hour pact duration.
- **Expel Vestige Feat** (*Tome of Magic*, p. 73):
  - Prerequisite: Soul binding.
  - 1/day, the binder can attempt to expel a bound vestige before 24 hours elapse.
  - The binder must redraw the vestige's seal and repeat the entire summoning ritual and binding check.
  - If the check succeeds, the vestige is expelled immediately and its slot freed.
  - Repercussion: Regardless of success or failure, the binder incurs a **-10 penalty on their next binding check with any vestige**, and an identical **-10 penalty on the binding check the next time they summon that specific expelled vestige**.

## Number of Simultaneously Bound Vestiges by Effective Binder Level
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 10, 11 (Table 1–1: The Binder), p. 18

| Effective Binder Level (EBL) | Maximum Simultaneous Vestiges |
| :---: | :---: |
| 1st – 7th | 1 |
| 8th – 13th | 2 |
| 14th – 19th | 3 |
| 20th | 4 |

- **Simultaneous Binding Rules**:
  - Each vestige occupies 1 unrestricted soul binding slot.
  - Each vestige must be summoned, bargained with, and bound individually (separate seal, separate full-round ritual, separate binding check).
  - Each binding check is evaluated independently for good vs. poor pact state.
  - The binder manifests the unique physical signs of all bound vestiges concurrently.
  - The binder possesses the combined granted abilities of all bound vestiges simultaneously.
  - If multiple poor pacts are active, the binder is subject to all active vestige influences at once; defiance penalties from any vestige stack with all others.

## Vestige Granted Abilities (Supernatural Abilities and Save DC: 10 + 1/2 EBL + CHA Modifier)
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 10–11, 19
- **Supernatural Nature (Su)**: Explicitly stated in the rules for granted abilities: "All powers granted by vestiges are supernatural in origin, even if they replicate spells or abilities that are not normally considered magical" (*Tome of Magic*, p. 19). Furthermore, "Abilities that duplicate the benefit of a feat do so even if the recipient does not qualify for the feat" (*Tome of Magic*, p. 19).
- **Activation & Components**:
  - Activating a granted ability is a standard action unless specifically described otherwise.
  - Granted abilities require no verbal, somatic, or material components, no divine focus, and no XP cost.
  - They incur no arcane armor spell failure chance, even when replicating arcane spells.
  - Activation does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
- **Magical Protections & Interactions**:
  - Supernatural abilities are not subject to Spell Resistance (SR).
  - They cannot be countered or dispelled by *dispel magic*.
  - They are suppressed within an *antimagic field*.
  - They affect incorporeal creatures normally, except that damaging abilities have a 50% miss chance against incorporeal targets if originating from a corporeal binder.
- **Caster Level**: If an ability duplicates a spell or shadow mystery, the caster level equals the binder's Effective Binder Level (CL = EBL) (*Tome of Magic*, p. 19).
- **Concentration**: No Concentration check is required to activate or maintain an ability unless expressly required by the ability description.
- **Saving Throw DC Formula**:
  $$\text{Save DC} = 10 + \left\lfloor\frac{1}{2}\text{Effective Binder Level}\right\rfloor + \text{Charisma modifier}$$

## 5-Round Cooldown Recharge for Vestige Abilities
- **Primary Source**: *Tome of Magic*, pp. 16, 19 ("Granted Abilities")
- **Ability Cadence**:
  - Constant/Passive: Abilities providing ongoing stat adjustments, immunities, or constant senses take effect immediately upon binding and remain continuously active.
  - At-Will: Minor utilitarian abilities without cooldown specifications can be used at will.
  - Active / High-Impact (5-Round Cooldown): Most non-constant active abilities (e.g., breath weapons, area bursts, blinding gazes, teleportation, lightning strikes) can be activated **only once every 5 rounds**.
- **Cooldown Timing & Tracking**:
  - When a 5-round cooldown ability is executed during round $R$, it cannot be used again until round $R + 5$ (e.g., activating in round 1 makes the ability available again in round 6).
  - On the combat timeline / round counter, this operates as a 5-round recovery timer tracked per ability.
  - Cooldowns are tracked independently for each ability; activating one ability does not place other abilities on cooldown unless explicitly stated in their descriptions.
- **Cooldown Modification**:
  - *Rapid Recovery Feat* (*Tome of Magic*, p. 74): A binder with Favored Vestige can select Rapid Recovery for that vestige, reducing the recharge cooldown for that vestige's abilities by 1 round (recharging once every 4 rounds instead of 5).
